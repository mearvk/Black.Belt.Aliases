I. The Occluded Investment Math and ORTs

In a lower-tier cognitive society, financial and investment algorithms are often
made public or left exposed to manipulation. This allows parasitic actors to
"game" the market, creating artificial crises, inflation, and wealth extraction.

  - The Occluded Math ("Notes to Selves"): To prevent this, the ultimate
    mathematical formulas governing long-term capital and temporal investment
    are occluded (hidden, restricted, or sealed within the auditing layer).
    These formulas are kept as "notes to selves" within the post-human Auditing
    matrix. The algorithms of systemic preservation are running in the
    background, invisible to the individual actor, ensuring that no single group
    can monopolize or weaponize the core mathematical flows of the planet.
  - The ORTs (Systemic Scraps and Localized Options): In contrast to the grand,
    occluded investment math, the ORTs (historically referring to "orts" or the
    remaining fragments, scraps, and localized options of a system) are left
    entirely open to the decentralized, everyday choices of individual actors.
    While the master macro-investments are occluded and secured, the localized
    micro-decisions—the remaining scraps of daily trade, local resource
    allocation, and personal choices—remain highly flexible and autonomous.

III. Sorting the Winners of the Educational Process

Education in this model is not merely a mechanism for job training; it is a
sovereign, self-contained process designed to treat "man as his own cause." The
"winners" of this educational process are those who demonstrate the highest
capacity for systems-thinking, moral stability, and vertical specialization.

Once these winners are identified, the system utilizes a precise, objective
sorting mechanism to route them away from generalized labor and into their
respective specialized processes:

1. INT Trades (Intellectual/Integration Systems)

Those who excel in high-level conceptual synthesis, philosophy, and systemic
auditing are routed to INT Trades. These actors are responsible for managing the
cognitive and informational architecture of the planet. They ensure that the
moral frameworks, communication networks, and administrative feedback loops of
the binary sort society remain clean and uncorrupted.

2. INVESTMENT Trades

Those who master the complex, long-term cycles of resource and temporal
distribution are routed to INVESTMENT Trades. While the absolute math remains
occluded, these specialists act as the stewards of generational capital. They
decide how resources are deployed to fund future technological prowess,
ecological balance, and the baseline welfare ("socialist treatment") of the next
generation.

3. CONTRACTS

Those who possess an absolute, uncompromising commitment to binary standards are
routed to CONTRACTS. This specialized pipeline manages the legal, structural,
and operational boundaries of the society. They ensure that mutual consent is
protected, that trade remains orderly, and that the binary distinctions of the
law are strictly enforced without bureaucratic gray areas.

This method of sorting creates a highly refined, cybernetic meritocracy. It

  - Alignment of Competence with Role: By sorting individuals into their

  - The Prevention of Systemic Decay: By separating macro-investment (which is

    prevents the Ouroboros from "consuming its own endtails." The social cloth

    for its actual, long-term future [1, 2].
