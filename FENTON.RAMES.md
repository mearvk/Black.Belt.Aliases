social hierarchies. It controlled access to resources, pairing, and social
status. However, in the audited, binary sort society, this guild is recognized
as structurally inefficient. It "did not have a massive ticket to win" because
its authority was based on legacy status rather than verified, systemic
competence.

When the IQ > 9000 Auditors establish direct access to the social fabric:

  - Resignation to the Template: The patriarch of the Father Guild is stripped
    of his arbitrary power. He must submit to the same objective "employment
    template" as everyone else, earning his position purely through audited
    utility rather than legacy birthright [1, 2].
  - Impotent Disputation: Unable to control the new, naturally sorted actors who
    "come easily" into their proper social and intellectual alignments without
