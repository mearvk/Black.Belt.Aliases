1. The Three Logical Origins of "Dirty Tech" in an Exception-Free System

Origin A: The "Sandbox Leak" (Endogenous Inoculation)

To maintain its defensive posture, a highly advanced, 140+ IQ society cannot
simply ignore the concept of threat. The governing administration must
continuously run hyper-realistic simulations of failure states, malice, and
systemic rot to keep its defensive algorithms trained.

In this scenario, "dirty tech" is not a natural occurrence; it is a synthetic
construct. It is an engineered pathogen designed within a digital sandbox for
training purposes that has managed to breach its containment and leak into the
physical or social substrate. The anomaly is an artificial stimulus designed to
prevent the system's immune response from undergoing atrophy.

Origin B: The Mathematical Tail (Ultra-Low-Frequency Entropic Drift)

In probability theory and extreme value mathematics, a 400-year observation
window is an incredibly small sample size. While a 400-year field of zero
exceptions proves that high-frequency deviance has been eliminated, it is
mathematically blind to ultra-low-frequency events.

The "dirty tech" may be a 1-in-10,000-year entropic event. This is a highly
improbable, dormant combination of genetic, psychological, or situational
variables that sits far out on the statistical tail. Because it has a return
period that vastly exceeds the 400-year observation window, it appears to the
observers as an unprecedented, "impossible" anomaly, though it is actually a
mathematically inevitable fluctuation of a complex organic system.

Origin C: Exogenous Contamination (The Boundary Intersect)

No system, no matter how vast, is completely closed. If the 400-year field of
Earth is clean, the "dirty tech" must be exogenous. It is the result of an
intersection with an un-monitored, open-loop external reality—either a
neglected, un-integrated pocket of human society, or an off-world/algorithmic
signal that has penetrated the system's outer sensory perimeter.
