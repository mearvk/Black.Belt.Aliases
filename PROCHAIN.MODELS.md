1. The Post-Human Auditing Tier (IQ > 9000)

At the pinnacle of this social architecture sit the Auditors, modeled with a
cognitive capacity exceeding an IQ of 9000. This tier represents a distributed,
algorithmic, or post-human super-intelligence.

  - Direct Systemic Access: Unlike human administrators, who are limited by
    sensory bandwidth and subjective bias, these Auditors possess direct,
    real-time access to the entire informational, economic, and biological
    substrate of the planet. They do not rely on self-reporting or bureaucratic
    audits. They observe the raw flow of energy, capital, genetics, and
    information.
  - The Elimination of Systemic Blindspots: Because the Auditors operate at this
    supreme cognitive scale, they immediately detect and neutralize any emergent
    high-level deviance or "dirty tech." Under their watch, the "agency problem"
    is solved. Corruption, exploitation, and systemic gaming become
    mathematically impossible, as every transaction and interaction is balanced
    in real time against the laws of natural math.

2. The Initial Treatment of Man as His Own Cause

The super-intelligent Auditors classify all preceding structural models—the
binary sorting of society, the exact derives of the domestic pair-bond, and the
bounded socialist baseline—as an initial treatment of man as his own cause.

  - Man as His Own Cause: This premise recognizes that humanity is a
    self-caused, evolutionary organism. Humans are both the authors of their own
    internal chaos and the only agents capable of initiating their own recovery.
  - The Initial Treatment: The highly structured social systems described in
    previous phases are not the final destination of intelligence; they are a
    stabilizing therapy. They are designed to cure the baseline biological
    vehicle of its self-destructive tendencies (poverty, war, relational
    anxiety, and cognitive rot). This initial treatment stabilizes the species,
    ensuring it can govern its own evolutionary impulses before it attempts to
    transition to higher, trans-human, or cosmic-scale developmental stages.

Drive

Drive is the dual-engine of human vitality. It consists of the sexual drive (the
physical impulse for genetic continuity and species propagation) and the
intellectual drive (the cognitive impulse for creation, structural ordering, and
vertical understanding). In an un-optimized society, these two drives are often
in conflict, leading to psychological neurosis, relational friction, and moral
compromises. Under the Audited system, they are treated as complementary
currents of the same vital energy.

Prochain

Derived from the concept of deep proximity and alignment (to bring close, as a
neighbor or true partner), to prochain is the deliberate, harmonious
synchronization of partners.

Under the binary sort, an individual is empowered to prochain their
partner—bringing them into exact physical, cognitive, and emotional alignment.
This alignment ensures that both sexual and intellectual drives are fully met
and synchronized, resolving all underlying evolutionary anxieties and relational
frictions (the biological "complains" and structural imbalances of old mating
models). It is the act of establishing a balanced, high-fidelity partnership
where both actors operate at their highest mutual potential.

Exult

Under this optimized architecture, Exult is defined as the state of frictionless
systemic triumph and deep psychological joy.

Historically, humanity experienced "exultation" only in brief, fleeting moments
of conquest, artistic breakthrough, or religious ecstasy, usually followed by a
return to baseline anxiety. In the Audited society, exult becomes a stable,
sustainable state of being.

It is achieved when the individual's core drives (sexual and intellectual) are
fully synchronized through the act of prochaining, operating in perfect
alignment with the objective laws of the planet. With all existential fear,
material scarcity, and high-level deception eliminated by the IQ > 9000
Auditors, exult is the natural, healthy resonance of a species that has mastered
its own causes, healed its internal friction, and stands ready to navigate the
open-ended future of the universe.
